Questions

Is it free?
Yes. No account, no ads, no download.
How accurate is the Gartua timer?
It is an estimate. We use Aion2Timers kill reports when they are fresh, otherwise the last time a trusted member set in Discord. Always check the Field Boss list in-game.
Why do my times differ from a friend's?
Each visitor sees times in their own device's time zone, so the clock times differ but the countdowns match.
Which regions are covered?
Asia is our home, so Gartua and server status are Asia only. Event schedules for the other regions are in the region menu.
How do I get alerts?
Join our Discord. Daeva posts Gartua and event alerts there, and you can choose which roles get pinged.
Why am I on the legion roster?
Only members who added themselves with /profile set appear, and /profile remove takes you off straight away. We only show what you entered, with links to your official and Armory pages.
Who runs this?
The Bajubesi Network community. It is an independent project, not affiliated with NCSOFT.
